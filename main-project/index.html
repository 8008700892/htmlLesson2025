Introducing the Game Boy

Fans of Nintendo's widely acclaimed video game releases such as Super Mario Bros, The Legend of Zelda, and Donkey Kong, can now enjoy that exciting Nintendo action anywhere with the new GAME BOY! A dot-matrix screen plus standard controls you'll recognize, Game boy is capable of playing a wide range of games using interchangeable cartridges. Powered with four "AA" batteries, the Game boy is conveniently sized to fit in your pocket and portable to take with you wherever you go.

Now you're playing with power... PORTABLE POWER!
